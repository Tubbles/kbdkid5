# Suggestions

Ideas noticed during work, not yet asked for. Strike or promote as you see fit.

- A fit test strip: a few short bosses, each with a horizontal flat-topped teardrop hole of a different diameter as modelled (say 4.3 to 4.7 in 0.05 steps), printed in the tray's orientation, to settle the snug (left) and slip (right) pin fits before printing two whole trays. The magnet slots could go on it too. `tray_shape.cut_teardrop_hole()` already makes the hole.
- A coupon like the nice!nano bay test strip (work item 006) for the north wall's three small mounts (the cradle with its lever slot, the reset back wall with its pin hole, the battery pocket), all snug fits on figures from drawings, not calipers.
- The battery header's hold against the plug's pull: with the header in hand, look for a step on its body behind the plug's housing that a pocket lip (`battery_shoulder`) could catch. Otherwise a printed strap over the pocket's top, across the header's back half, or glue.
- Socket strip pin holes past 3.54 (`socket_test_shape.py`): merge the tab recess into the pin hole on purpose instead of leaving a web under `RECESS_HOLE_CLEARANCE_MM` 0.3 (export_socket_test.py). The south tab's recess starts at the body's end, 2.07 from the pin hole at footprint (-5.95, 0.0), so it keeps 0.37 from the 3.4 hole, 0.32 from the 3.5 one, 0.12 at 3.9 and merges at 4.14. A web that thin as modelled prints as a sliver, and the recess and the hole belong to the same contact, so a merge costs nothing. On the lip strip lobe B's south finger's slots would have to open into the hole at footprint (-3.75, 5.0) likewise, as the north finger's room already opens into the center post's: they keep `FINGER_SLOT_HOLE_WEB_MM` 0.25 only up to 3.44 (0.27 at 3.4, 0.02 at 3.9). The hole strip settled the frame's pin holes at 3.2 (doc/log/2026-10-18.md), so this matters only for a strip that still wants holes past 3.54.
