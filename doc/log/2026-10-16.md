# 2026-10-16

- #repo Started kbdkid5 from kbdkid4's 3D-print tooling (FreeCAD scripts, the kbdkid3 plate model, the headless FreeCAD image), CC0 like kbdkid4. No LibrePCB project yet: the user designs the PCB later.
- #layout The geometry lives in `scripts/layout.py` (pure Python, no FreeCAD) until a PCB exists, since there is no STEP to read the board from. Keys, outline and mounting drills come from kbdkid4's board.
- #slant The user's slant: from the thumb key's top right corner to the rightmost top row key's top right corner, so the halves join into one board. Joined, the rows dip toward the middle, like an Alice (the user corrected my first claim that it angles the other way). 17.74 degrees per half, against 6 to 10 for common Alice boards.
- #battery Same battery as kbdkid4 (user): a 34.37 x 51.53 x 4.11 pouch with a 66 mm lead and a JST PH plug from a corner. Under the sockets it needs the PCB at least 6.63 above the floor.
- #mounting kbdkid4's mounting drill at (18.574, 56.832) moved to (18.574, 37.782), out from under the nice!nano.
- #parts SS-12D00-G3 power switch, PTS636 SL43 LFS reset button, JST B2B-PH-K-S battery header, from the research in doc/components.md. The other candidates were under-rated for the 500 mA charge current, or had SMD terminals that are hard to air-wire.
- #joint Magnets alone cannot carry lap use in a face this low (doc/components.md, section 5), so two hardened 4 x 24 dowel pins carry the bending and the magnets only close the joint.
- #height The PCB stays 8.0 above the floor, as on kbdkid4 (user: margin for padding on this first iteration), not the 6.63 the battery would allow. With the taller block under the PCB, the joint takes five 8 x 3 magnets per half instead of six 6 x 3, in slots open at the top so they drop in.
- #joint Dowel pins 4 x 16 instead of 4 x 24 (user: 12 mm sticking out in split mode is long, and no hand will rest on the joint). 8 embedded per half keeps a keystroke at about 6 MPa on the plastic.
- #joint The dowel pins are loose (user), so nothing sticks out in split mode. Each half parks one pin in its thumb edge, held by a 4 x 2 magnet, 1.5 proud to pull out.
- #mirror The right half is the left one mirrored across x = 66.199, the outline's center line (`layout.RIGHT_HALF_MIRROR_X_MM`), one line for every export. Not each part's own bounding box center as on kbdkid4, since the plate and the tray reach past the outline by different amounts, and not x = 0, so a right part sits where its left one does.
- #layout check_layout.py found four conflicts in the first DESIGN.md: the reset stem, the battery and the PCB connector zone in the ledge, and five magnet slots that cannot clear the dowel bosses. Fixed by cutting the ledge at the button, moving the battery 2.2 north, shrinking the zone, and four magnets between the pins, which moved toward the slant's ends (48.5 apart, better against twist). The magnet slots also needed the wall above them cut back to pass the magnet.
- #plate The plate is built by `scripts/plate_shape.py`, a module without an entry point, and exported by `scripts/export_plate.py`, so the next mock (PCB mock and plate in one print) can import `build_plate()`. freecadcmd scripts cannot be imported, since they leave through `os._exit`. kbdkid4's case export shares the tray through its `tray_shape.py` the same way.
- #plate kbdkid4 trims its plate 0.4 (`PLATE_EDGE_TRIM_MM` in its `export_plate.py`), as DESIGN.md says. kbdkid4 trims the cell compound with one `common()`, which kbdkid4 itself found can drop a cell on FreeCAD 1.0, so here the trim goes solid by solid and counts the cells.
- #plate The corner slab stops at the thumb cell's east edge. kbdkid4's corner reached its outline plus 0.35 eastward, with a tab below it out to the same reach beside its nice!nano. kbdkid5 has no such region, and a slab out to the outline plus 0.35 there would stick out 0.27 past the thumb cell, a nub the side shave would cut to 0.17. The plate's east edge is now the thumb cell's, 0.024 inside the outline after the shave, like its west edge.
- #plate #layout kbdkid5 places no diodes yet, so the covered plate's pit takes kbdkid4's place for them: every left-half diode on kbdkid4's board sits 4.28 north of its key, rotated 180 degrees (`layout.DIODE_OFFSET_FROM_KEY_MM`). check_layout.py gets no check for it: it places nothing but the pit, whose fit in the cover the plate export checks.
- #plate The export checks the plate against the tray's cavity as the outline moved out 0.4 with round corners. kbdkid4's tray rounds the cavity's convex corners with at most that and its concave ones outward, so the real cavity only has more room. The slanted edge is the closest.
- #plate The side shave also moves the plate's slanted edge in by 0.1, along its normal, so it ends 0.25 past the slant and 0.15 from the cavity wall. The slab is still built 0.35 past the slant. Shaving only the x extremes left the slanted edge 0.05 from the wall, where kbdkid4's east edge started before its printed plate came out a snug fit and got the shave (kbdkid4 commit 186266d, "Shave 0.1 mm off the plate's west and east edges"), which left it 0.15 from the wall. The plate export now checks the slanted edge's offset (0.25) and the least clearance to the cavity wall (0.15) against DESIGN.md figures transcribed in the script, not against the constants the plate is built from.
